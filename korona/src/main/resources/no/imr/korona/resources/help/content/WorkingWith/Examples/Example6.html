Example 6 - School categorisation

The identification of acoustic categories is improved if the variance is reduced, e.g. by assuming that a school contains one species only. The school categorisation module splits schools into segments, and compares those to investigate if the school is likely to contain one or several species. The SchoolCategorisationModule has to be used after the CategorisationModule as shown in the first figure of Example 5. The results are similar, except that whole schools receive the same acoustic category.
