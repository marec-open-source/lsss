Track categorization module

Module for performing categorization of whole tracks.

Requirements

  • Track datagrams, Tbr0Datagram and Tnf0Datagram, see TrackingModule.

New datagrams

  • A track categorization datagram for each detected track.

Parameters

  • Active: If the module is not active, then all datagrams pass unaltered through it.

References

  • Pedersen, G., and Korneliussen, R. J. 2009. The relative frequency response derived from individually separated targets of northeast Arctic cod (Gadus morhua), saithe (Pollachius virens), and Norway pout (Trisopterus esmarkii). – ICES Journal of Marine Science, 66: 1149–1154.
