EK80 transducer impedance

The EK80 system encodes measurements of transmitted (complex) voltage and current for each sector in the beginning of each ping. Thus, it is possible to calculate the impedance per ping/transducer sector i by

Zi = |Vi / Ii| [Ω]

where V is the transmitted voltage, and I is the transmitted current. The numbering of the sectors depends on the transducer and follows the same convention as in the computation of the split beam angles. The impedance is calculated per sample, from the start of each ping, up to the sample corresponding to the end of the sent pulse, yielding an array holding the temporal evolution of impedance during pulse transmission.

For narrowband data, the average of the central part of the array associated with the current sector and ping is used. For broadband data, the impedance at the time of transmission of the center frequency of the used frequency band is used.
