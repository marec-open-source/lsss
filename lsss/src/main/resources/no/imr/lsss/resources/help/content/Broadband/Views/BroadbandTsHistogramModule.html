BB TS(f) histogram

This view shows a histogram of targets detected by BB TS(f).

The frequency×TS-plane is discretized into uniformly sized cells. The cell size along the frequency axis is Frequency resolution configured in BB TS(f). Each cell counts the number of targets whose TS(f) curve intersects that cell.

The colour of a cell is given by a gray scale where

  • White represents a count of zero
  • Black represents the highest count of all cells

Settings

  • TS resolution: The cell size along the TS axis.
  • Auto-adjust axes: Automatically adjust the x-axis and y-axis when the plot is updated.
