Large Scale Survey System

Large Scale Survey System, abbreviated LSSS, and pronounced L-triple-S, is a system for interpreting multi-frequency echosounder data. The system also supports other data types to supplement the acoustic data in the interpretation process. Interpretation can be done either offshore during a survey or later onshore as postprocessing.

The interpretation process consists of defining regions on the echogram and assigning acoustic categories to each region. The regions with assignments are then used for computing a coarse representation of the interpretation on a rectangular grid that is stored to a database.
