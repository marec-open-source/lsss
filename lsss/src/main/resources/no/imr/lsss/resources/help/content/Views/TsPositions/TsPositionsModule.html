TS positions

This module displays the angles of:

  • Targets detected by TS distributions, and by BB TS(f).
    Targets close to the mouse position in the echogram are highlighted in red.
  • Selected tracks, but not the visually edited parts.
    The positions of a track are colored red and connected with lines.
  • Optionally, the echogram sample at the mouse position.

Settings

  • Cross: Show cross.
  • Angular lines: Show angular lines.
  • Angular text: Show angular text.
  • Cartesian axes labels: Show Cartesian axes labels.
  • Cartesian grid lines: Show Cartesian grid lines.
  • Cartesian grid text: Show Cartesian grid text.
  • Mouse position: Show angles at mouse position.
