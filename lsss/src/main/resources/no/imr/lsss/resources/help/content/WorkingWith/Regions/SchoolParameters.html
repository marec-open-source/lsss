School parameters

A number of parameters are calculated for each school. These parameters are saved to work files, and can be exported. They can also be shown in the echogram tooltip.

To enable calculation of school parameters, go to [Survey configuration - Miscellaneous] and make sure the "Compute school parameters" option is selected. The school parameters are recomputed whenever a school is modified. All pings are used when calculating the school parameters.

The calculation of school parameters uses masking this way:

  • Morphological parameters, such as length and area, do not use any kind of masking or thresholding.
  • Value-based parameters, such as sA and mean sv, use exclusion and frequency-dependent deletion, but do not use conditional masking or thresholding.

The school parameters include the flag "Data processed" signalling whether the parameters were computed using original or processed data. The parameters are computed using the currently selected data and are not automatically recomputed when switching between original and processed data. To recompute all school parameters, go to [Survey configuration - Miscellaneous] and click the "Recompute" button.

Frequency independent parameters

These parameters do not depend on the data of specific frequencies.

  • Area: The area of the school.
  • Bottom depth: The bottom depth at the ping where Min dist bottom is found.
  • Circumference: The circumference of the school.
  • Corrected height: Height - effectivePulseDuration × soundVelocity.
  • Corrected length: Length - {half the beam width for center depth on the first ping} - {half the beam width for center depth on the last ping}.
  • Height: The maximum per ping vertical extent of the school.
  • Length: The horizontal extent of the school.
  • Max depth: The maximum depth of the school.
  • Min depth: The minimum depth of the school.
  • Min dist bottom: The minimum distance from the deepest part of the school to coordinated bottom.

The Corrected height and Corrected length use data at the configured main frequency.

Per frequency parameters

These parameters are computed for each frequency.

  • sA: Always calculated using vessel distance, and does not depend on the chosen horizontal grid unit.
  • Sample count
  • sv statistics:
    • Kurtosis: The fourth standardized moment, a measure of "tailedness".
    • Max: The maximum value.
    • Mean: The average value.
    • Mean, truncated x%: The x% lowest and the x% highest values are discarded before computing the mean.
    • Median: The median value.
    • Min: The minimum value.
    • Skewness: The third standardized moment, a measure of asymmetry.
    • Variance: The variance.
