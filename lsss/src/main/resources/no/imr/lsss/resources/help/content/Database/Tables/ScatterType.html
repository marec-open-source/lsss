ScatterType

Name

ScatterType - List of scatter data types

Synopsis

struct ScatterType {
  short ScatterType;
  char  ScatterTypeName[40];
};

Description

This table contains a mapping between a scatter type code and the scatter type name.

Parameters

  • ScatterType - Scatter type code.
  • ScatterTypeName - Name of scatter type. Currently, the following choices are available:
    1000 = 'scatter_pelagic' //Data from echogram-object in pelagic channels, i.e. parallel to surface. The pelagic echogram depth-channels 'scatter_pelagic' starts at the sea-surface and continue down to the lower integration line which is defined by the operator, but which is commonly just above the bottom (e.g. 0.5 m). The sea surface is defined relative to the transducer face, which could be e.g. 9.3 m above a transducer mounted on a protruding instrument-keel of a research vessel. The lower integration line is defined by the operator. Note that UpperDepth, LowerDepth, UpperInterpretationDepth and LowerInterpretationDepth express limitations on where data may be collected from. The deepest value of UpperDepth and UpperInterpretationDepth is used, and the shallowest value of LowerDepth and LowerInterpretationDepth. Depth channel 1 starts at the sea surface, channel 2 starts where channel 1 stops, etc. Channel 0 is a special sum channel designed to cover all pelagic channels. Summing up the sA values from all the pelagic channels for a particular acoustic category produces the sA value of channel 0 for that acoustic category.
    2000 = 'scatter_bottom' //Data from echogram-object in bottom-related channels, i.e. parallel to bottom. The bottom channels are organised in a similar manner as the pelagic depth-channels and starts at the bottom and increase with increasing distance from the bottom. Channel 1 is next to the bottom, channel 2 starts where channel 1 stops, etc., and channel 0 is a special channel designed to cover all bottom channels. The bottom channels starts from the detected bottom selected by the operator. This may be the bottom detected at 38 kHz, the bottom coordinated between many frequencies. The bottom channel terminates at the surface. Further, the data used is limited by UpperDepth, LowerDepth, UpperInterpretationDepth and LowerInterpretationDepth set for the channel type. The UpperIntegrationDepth is commonly 15 m below the sea surface, and the LowerInterpretationDepth is commonly 0.5 m above the detected bottom.
    3000 = 'school_pelagic' //Data from school-object in pelagic channels. The pelagic school channels 'school_pelagic' are defined exactly as the echogram school channels 'scatter_pelagic', but is limited by the extent of the school region. The upper and lower extents of the data used are indicated by UpperInterpretationDepth and LowerInterpretationDepth or by UpperDepth and LowerDepth if those give a smaller extent.
    4000 = 'school_bottom' //Data from school-object in bottom-related channels. The bottom school channels are defined exactly as the echogram school channels 'scatter_bottom', but is limited by the extent of the school region. The upper and lower extents of the data used are indicated by UpperInterpretationDepth and LowerInterpretationDepth or by UpperDepth and LowerDepth if those give a smaller extent.
